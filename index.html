The five stages of diabetic retinopathy

The International Clinical DR (ICDR) scale used by APTOS 2019 and EyePACS. Grades 0–3 are non-proliferative (NPDR). Grade 4 is proliferative (PDR), where new abnormal vessels grow.

Based on Atwany, Sahyoun & Yaqub, “Deep Learning Techniques for Diabetic Retinopathy Classification: A Survey”, IEEE Access, 2022.
